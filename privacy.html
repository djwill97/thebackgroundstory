Privacy Policy

Background Story Uploader · Last updated November 9, 2026

Background Story Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of The Background Story YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner to manage their own channel.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload and manage videos on their YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl). It uses this access only to upload videos, Shorts, thumbnails and subtitles and to edit their titles, descriptions and settings on The Background Story channel.

What is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data or YouTube data to anyone else, and it does not use any data for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
